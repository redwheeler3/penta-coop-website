Penta Housing Application System

Applications for households and a private review workspace for the membership committee

What it is

The Penta Housing Application System includes the applicant experience at applications.pentacoop.com and a private committee workspace at screener.pentacoop.com. Committee access is limited to authorized members.

What it does

Applicants save and submit their information directly in Penta's application service. The system:

  • keeps private working copies and dated submissions;
  • tracks which current openings each household enters;
  • applying the co-op's eligibility rules (such as household size and income ranges);
  • helping committee members organize, compare, and prioritize applications into a shortlist for the committee to consider.

The tool supports the committee's work; it does not make membership decisions. Final decisions are always made by people, following the co-op's bylaws and process.

How it uses Google sign-in

Applicants and authorized committee members may sign in with their Google account for identity only. Signing in does not give Penta access to their Google Drive, Gmail, or other Google data.

For full detail on what information the app collects and how it is used, see our Privacy Policy.
