Penta Application Screener

An internal review tool for the Penta Housing Co-op membership committee

What it is

The Penta Application Screener is a private, internal web application used by the membership committee of Penta Housing Co-Operative Association to review applications for membership in the co-op. It is not a public service; access is limited to authorized committee members, who sign in at screener.pentacoop.com.

What it does

When people apply to join the co-op, their responses are collected in a Google Sheet. The screener helps the committee work through those applications by:

  • reading the submitted applications from the co-op's Google Sheet;
  • applying the co-op's eligibility rules (such as household size and income ranges);
  • helping committee members organize, compare, and prioritize applications into a shortlist for the committee to consider.

The tool supports the committee's work; it does not make membership decisions. Final decisions are always made by people, following the co-op's bylaws and process.

How it uses Google sign-in

Committee members sign in with their Google account for identity only. Signing in does not give the app access to a member's Google Drive, Gmail, or other Google data. Separately, an authorized administrator connects the single Google Sheet of application responses using Google's file picker, which grants read-only access to that one file so the committee can review submissions.

For full detail on what information the app collects and how it is used, see our Privacy Policy.
